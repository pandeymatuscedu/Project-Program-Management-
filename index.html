D3. StockBridge: Agentic Inventory Prototype

Prototype, iterating
USC Marshall AI Product Showdown, Oct 2026, 4-person team
Scope
Working prototype for a 300-SKU catalog across 2 regions and 6 channels: messy stocklist ingestion, SKU matching, demand forecasting, order recommendations, receiving, and human-approved marketplace updates.
Design
Human-in-the-loop matching and exception workflow using UPC, model code, and title similarity, with Claude only for unclear lines.
Outcome
290 of 400 rows (72.5%) matched without AI, 110 flagged for review. Modeled a 2.9x conservative payback. Delivered a 5-minute pitch with a 90-second live demo and placed 3rd of 15 teams.
Open live prototype
